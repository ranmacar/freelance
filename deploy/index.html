About Me

Born in Nitra, Slovakia, I'm a passionate developer with over 15 years of experience building web applications and exploring cutting-edge technologies. My journey spans from C++ development to modern React applications and beyond, with a special focus on AI integration and sustainable technology solutions.

I've worked across diverse industries - from payroll systems in Austria to mobile apps for major German media companies, and most recently Carbon Farming and contributing to LiteFarm.

My interests lie at the intersection of technology and sustainability, including AI, renewable energy, AgTech, and water efficiency solutions. Beyond pure development, I explore libertarian philosophy through my Geekship platform, work on alternative innovation systems through SOIL (Scalable Open Innovation Lab), and create content for Arbolis - a TV show and book project that ties it together.

I'm always excited to take on projects that combine technical excellence with positive impact!
